Privacy Policy

Short version: no accounts, no cookies, no tracking, and we don't keep what you type.

Last updated: 26 October 2026

In short: you can use AeroPredict without an account. We don't use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers, and we don't save the flight details you enter.

1. Who we are

AeroPredict ("we", "us") is an independent website that estimates flight delay risk and offers airport tools. You can reach us through our contact page.

2. Information you enter

To give you an answer, the details you type are sent to our server: for example flight numbers, airport codes, dates and times, the place you're starting from, your gate, travel preferences and what happened to your flight. We use them only to work out your answer. We don't save them in a database, don't link them to you, and don't sell or share them.

Please don't enter information you don't need to, such as your name or booking reference. None of our tools ask for it.

3. Technical information

Like every website, our hosting provider receives technical information when your browser connects, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, and may keep it in short-lived server logs for security and troubleshooting. We use your IP address briefly, in memory only, to limit how many requests one visitor can make, so the service stays available for everyone. It isn't stored or used to identify you.

4. Cookies and tracking

We don't set cookies, don't store anything in your browser, and don't use analytics, advertising or social-media trackers. If we ever add privacy-friendly visitor statistics, we will update this page first.

5. Services your browser loads

To display the site, your browser loads a few files directly from other providers, which receive your IP address as part of that request:

  • Google Fonts (typefaces)
  • jsDelivr and cdnjs (charting and map software libraries)
  • OpenStreetMap tile servers (the map on Near My Gate)

6. Public data we look up for you

To answer some requests, our server looks up public information using only flight and airport details, never your personal data or IP address: airport weather forecasts (aviationweather.gov), airport facilities (OpenStreetMap services) and, where enabled, flight schedules (AeroDataBox).

7. Compensation partners

If you click a link such as “Claim it for me”, you leave AeroPredict for an independent claims company. The link tells them you came from us, so we can earn a commission. Anything you share on their site is covered by their own privacy policy, not this one.

8. Your rights

Because we don't keep personal information, there is usually nothing for us to access, correct or delete. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or UK GDPR) you have rights over your personal data, and you can contact us with any question or request.

9. Children

AeroPredict is meant for a general audience and doesn't knowingly collect information from children.

10. Changes

If we change how we handle information, we will update this page and the date above.
