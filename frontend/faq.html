Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about predictions, coverage and your rights.

Is AeroPredict free?
Yes. Checking flights and using every tool on the site is free, with no account needed.
How accurate are the predictions?
We test them on real flights from months that were kept out of the data, and publish the results. In short: they are clearly better than guessing, and the percentages match what really happens on average, but no one can predict every delay. See How it works and the Analytics page for the figures.
What does a 25% chance of delay mean?
Out of every four flights like yours, about one arrives more than 15 minutes late. It doesn't mean your flight will be 25% late, and a low number is not a guarantee.
Does AeroPredict show my flight's live status?
No. We estimate the odds from past records and the weather forecast. For your flight's real status on the day, check your airline's app or the airport's departure board.
Why does my result say “Estimate only”?
We don't have records for that flight, its route or its airports yet, so the number is a general estimate from the time of day and season. We add new countries and airports regularly.
Which flight number should I enter?
The one on your booking, for example EK384 or BA117: the airline's two-character code followed by the number. If your ticket shows a partner airline's number (a codeshare), the operating airline's number gives the most accurate result.
Which countries and airlines do you cover?
Real records cover 138 countries, over 900 airports and over 150 airlines, with the most detail for the US, UK, Brazil, South Korea, Australia, Hong Kong, Norway, Israel, Taiwan and South Africa. See the full list.
My flight was delayed or cancelled. Am I owed money?
Possibly. In the EU and UK you can be owed €250–€600 for a delay of three hours or more, unless it was caused by extraordinary circumstances such as severe weather. Other countries have their own rules. Use My Rights to check your flight.
How does “Claim it for me” work?
It takes you to an independent compensation company that can file the claim with the airline for you, usually for a share of the compensation and nothing if the claim fails. We may earn a commission from them at no cost to you. You can always claim directly from the airline yourself, for free.
Do you store my searches or personal data?
No accounts, no cookies and no tracking. What you type is used to work out your answer and isn't saved in a database. Details are in our privacy policy.
Are you connected to an airline?
No. AeroPredict is independent and isn't affiliated with or endorsed by any airline, airport or government agency.
I found a mistake. How do I report it?
Please contact us with the flight, the date and what looks wrong.
